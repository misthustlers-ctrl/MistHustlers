The Open Hearth

At Ember & Oak, our heart beat is the fire. We believe that the most authentic flavors are unlocked through the ancient dance of wood and flame. Our custom-built hearth isn't just a kitchen tool; it's the anchor of our home, radiating a warmth that permeates every dish and every conversation.
